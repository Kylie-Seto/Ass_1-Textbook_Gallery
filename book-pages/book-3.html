The Curious Writer

Brief Fourth Edition

Overview

The Curious Writer by Bruce Ballenger introduces students to academic writing through inquiry, reflection, and critical thinking. Rather than focusing solely on rules, the book encourages writers to explore ideas, ask meaningful questions, and engage thoughtfully with texts and real-world issues. It covers key writing concepts such as argument, research, revision, and rhetorical awareness, while emphasizing writing as a process. Practical examples and student-friendly activities help readers develop confidence and clarity in their writing. This text is well suited for first-year college students learning to write across academic disciplines.
