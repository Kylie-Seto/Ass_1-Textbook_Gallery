Starting Out With C++ Early Objects

Eighth Edition

Overview

Starting Out with C++: Early Objects by Tony Gaddis, Judy Walters, and Godfrey Muganda introduces programming concepts through a clear and accessible approach to C++. The book emphasizes problem solving and program design while gradually introducing object-oriented principles. Topics include variables, control structures, functions, classes, and basic data structures. Step-by-step examples and practice exercises help students build confidence as they learn to write and understand C++ programs. This text is ideal for beginners and students taking their first course in computer programming.
