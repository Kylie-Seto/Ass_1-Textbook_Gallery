The Economic Way Of Thinking

Thirteenth Edition

Overview

The Economic Way of Thinking by Paul Heyne, Peter Boettke, and David Prychitko introduces students to economic reasoning as a way of understanding everyday decisions and social issues. Rather than focusing only on formulas, the book emphasizes how incentives, trade-offs, and human behavior shape economic outcomes. It presents core economic principles through real-world examples and accessible explanations. By encouraging critical thinking, the text helps readers apply economic concepts to both personal and societal decision-making. This book is well suited for introductory economics and social science courses.
