E-commerce 2013

business. technology. society.

Overview

E-commerce 2013: Business, Technology, Society provides a comprehensive introduction to the rapidly evolving world of online commerce. The book explores how digital technologies, platforms, and business models shape modern markets and consumer behavior. Key topics include electronic payments, security and privacy, user experience design, and the social impact of e-commerce systems. Through real-world examples and case studies, the authors connect technical concepts with practical business strategy. This text is ideal for students seeking a broad foundation in e-commerce within a global and societal context.
