Global Marketing Management

Eighth Edition

Overview

Global Marketing Management by Warren J. Keegan examines how organizations develop and implement marketing strategies in an increasingly interconnected world. The book explores cultural, economic, political, and legal factors that influence global markets and consumer behavior. Key topics include market entry strategies, global branding, pricing, distribution, and communication across international boundaries. Through real-world examples and case studies, students learn how companies adapt marketing practices to different regions and cultures. This text is ideal for students studying international business and global marketing strategy.
